# MogVault

MogVault is a Dalamud plugin for Final Fantasy XIV that remembers what your characters own. It saves your data in the background while you play, so you can look things up later from any character without logging in on the others.

* Item search across inventory, armoury chest, saddlebags, retainers and free company chests of every character
* Vendor value of everything you hold
* /mogvault find followed by an item name searches from chat
* Item tooltips can show where you keep an item and how many you have
* Character overview with free company, job, main scenario progress and gil
* Job levels, quests, currencies and collections like mounts and minions compared across all characters
* Glamour dresser and armoire contents of every character
* Retainers with gil, venture timer and a list of everything they hold
* Free companies with rank, Grand Company standing, members, chest gil and credits
* Submersible and airship return timers
* Houses of your characters and free companies with the last time you went inside, so no estate gets demolished by surprise
* Daily backups that stay on your computer
* Delete single characters and free companies or wipe all saved data
* Hide names option that replaces every name with an invented one for screenshots
* IPC for other plugins, see docs/MogVaultIpc.cs
